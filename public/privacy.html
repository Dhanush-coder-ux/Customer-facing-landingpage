DOCUMENT 4 — ShopiQ Privacy Policy

ShopiQ Privacy Policy

Version 2.1 · Effective from 7 October 2026 · Operating Entity: InventQ

Statutory Compliance: Prepared in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 · Information Technology Act, 2000 and SPDI Rules, 2011 · Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020/2026.

1. Introduction

This policy explains what personal information ShopiQ collects when you use the app, how we use it, and your statutory rights. The platform is operated by InventQ with its registered office at 19, Punugu Asari Street South gate Madurai 625001.

Please read section 5 carefully — it explains what information is shared with the local retailers you buy from.

2. What we collect and why

Information Category What We Use It For
Name Creating your account, placing orders, delivery identification
Mobile number Signing you in via OTP, order updates, retailer fulfillment contact
Email address (optional) Account notices, receipts, support communications, marketing if consented
Password Securing your account — stored hashed (bcrypt), never visible to us

Payment Information: Payments are made directly to the retailer. ShopiQ does not collect, process, or store your debit/credit card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank account credentials.

Location Information: Location access may be precise or approximate depending on your device permission. Location access is completely optional and the app works smoothly without it. You can change this at any time in your device settings.

3. How we determine the purposes for processing

Depending on the activity, we process personal data under lawful grounds:

  • To provide a service you have requested, including creating your account and passing your order to the retailer;
  • With your consent, where consent is required — for example, marketing messages or location access;
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations;
  • For security, fraud prevention and protection of our services and users, where permitted by law; and
  • For other purposes permitted by applicable Indian law.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To transmit your orders to the retailer and keep you updated on their status
  • To show you shops and products near you
  • To respond to your questions and complaints
  • To send essential notices about your orders and account
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and misuse of the platform
  • To improve the app, using aggregated and de-identified information
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information under any circumstances.

5. Information shared with retailers

When you place an order, we provide the retailer with the personal information reasonably necessary to fulfill and administer that order:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your delivery address
  • The items and quantities you ordered
The retailer is an independent Data Fiduciary:

After receiving your information, the retailer independently determines how it handles that information for order fulfillment, delivery, invoicing, returns, refunds, and legal compliance. The retailer is responsible for its own processing under applicable law.

We share your details only with retailers you actually order from. Browsing a store or checking availability does NOT share anything.

6. Other sharing

We share information with vetted service providers who help us operate the platform (cloud hosting, database clusters, SMS/OTP gateways, and crash diagnostic tools) under strict confidentiality commitments.

Some providers may process information outside India. Where that happens, it is done with appropriate safeguards in accordance with Indian law. We may also disclose data where required by valid legal process.

7. Communications and notifications

Transactional communications (order confirmations, delivery updates, security alerts) are necessary for order fulfillment and cannot be disabled.

Marketing communications are sent only with your consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time via in-app settings or the unsubscribe option.

8. Reviews

Reviews you post are public and display your chosen display name. Do not include private phone numbers or personal details in review text.

8A. Sensitive personal data (SPDI Rules 2011)

Under the Information Technology (SPDI) Rules, 2011, sensitive information includes passwords, financial data, health records, and biometric data.

The only sensitive category we hold is your password, which is stored in irreversible hashed form (bcrypt) and is never visible to us. We do not collect or store your card, bank, or UPI credentials — payments are made directly to the retailer — and we do not collect health or biometric data.

9. Security

We protect your information using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption in transit, encryption at rest, hashed passwords, restricted and logged internal access, and session expiry.

10. Personal data breaches

We maintain documented procedures for detecting, assessing, containing, investigating, and responding to personal data breaches, and will notify the Data Protection Board and affected users within required statutory timeframes.

11. Your rights under DPDP Act, 2023

  • Access: Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Nomination: Nominate an individual to exercise these rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Grievance: Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer.

To exercise your rights, email shopper@gmail.com. If your request concerns information held independently by a retailer, we will provide their details and assist you in submitting your request.

12. Data retention schedule

  • Account information: Retained while your account is active.
  • Order history: Retained for 24 months to support returns, disputes, and your reference.
  • Security and audit logs: Retained for 12 months.
  • Registration information: Retained for 180 days post account closure pursuant to Rule 3(1)(h) of the IT Rules 2021 before permanent deletion.

13. Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. Accounts created by minors will be suspended and deleted.

14. Cookies and mobile SDKs

On our website, we use necessary cookies for authentication and security. In our mobile applications, we use device identifiers and SDKs for authentication, security, and crash reporting.
